About Us

Our Mission, Vision & Core Values

Learn about Innovarch Design and Projects Consult Ltd — a leading Nigerian consultancy built on excellence, integrity, and innovation.

Innovarch Design and Projects Consult Ltd. (IDPC) is a private professional service company incorporated in Nigeria on 19th September 2022. We are a multi-sectoral firm delivering architectural, engineering, and project consultancy services with an unwavering focus on quality, innovation, and client satisfaction. From concept to completion, IDPC partners with clients to turn vision into reality.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, high-quality design and project management services to our clients, while delivering exceptional customer service and value. We strive to exceed our clients' expectations and help them achieve their goals through our expertise and dedication to sustainability.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of design and project management services in our industry, recognized for our innovation, expertise, and commitment to sustainability. We envision a future where we have helped our clients achieve their goals and create a positive impact on the world through our services.
